# Changelog

Every release of Eternal Survivors, newest first. Each lists its patch notes (gameplay, balance,
quality of life) and its changelog (fixes and details).

## v0.5.5

A small balance update.

### Patch notes

**Balance**
- **Fan of Knives** (Rogue legendary amulet) now grows when you rank it up: each cast has a 10% chance of a third ring of knives, 0.5 sec after the second, and that chance grows with each rank. Before, ranking it up did nothing.

## v0.5.4

A small balance and fixes update.

### Patch notes

**Gameplay**
- **Blade Dance** (Mortal Strike legendary) no longer dashes: each strike now sends two ghostly reflections of your hero that run ahead, then chase and spin through enemies for 5 seconds. Their total damage is the same as before.
- **Warband allies** who fight in melee now move around their enemy while they strike, like real players, instead of standing still. Tanks still hold their ground while they taunt.
- **Warsong Gulch:** enemy rogues and cat druids run a little faster (6.8 yards a second).

**Balance**
- **Cooldown cards for damage and speed buffs** (Tiger's Fury, Power Infusion, Battle Shout, Adrenaline Rush, Slice and Dice, Perception, Blood Fury, Berserking and Arcane Power) take 30% less off: 5 / 7 / 9 / 12 / 14% instead of 7 / 10 / 13 / 17 / 20%. Other spells' cooldown cards are unchanged.

**Quality of life**
- **Codex:** rings, trinkets and necklaces, ordinary and legendary, are added to the codex as soon as a card shows them, taken or not, Challenge Mode included.

### Changelog

**Fixes**
- Some creatures could show an old, wrong name (an Eye Tentacle named "Bear"); the saved name list now starts afresh after each update.
- Less slowdown when some warband allies fight big packs.
- Boss timer bars and warnings no longer stay on the warband screen when you end a run during a boss fight.

## v0.5.3

A weapon balance update.

### Patch notes

**Balance**
- **Weapon loadouts reworked:** every loadout now has two clear upsides and one downside, with smaller and steadier bonuses (10%, 5% or 2%). Two-handers keep their double swing damage; swing speeds, Auto Shot speeds and the 30% shield block are unchanged. No loadout is now more than about 8% stronger or weaker than its class's average.
- **Warrior:** Two-hander +10% rage per swing and +5% crit damage, -10% max health. Two one-handers +5% swing speed and +10% crit damage, -10% damage. One-hander and shield +10% max health and +10% damage struck back, -5% damage.
- **Paladin:** One-hander and off-hand +5% crit and +10% heal and shield power, -5% max health. One-hander and shield +10% max health and +10% damage struck back, -10% damage. Two-hander +5% damage, -5% max health.
- **Hunter:** Bow +5% move speed and +2% cooldown recovery, -10% Auto Shot damage. Crossbow +10% crit damage and +10% damage to elites and bosses, -5% move speed. Gun +5% crit and +10% crit damage, -10% pet damage.
- **Rogue:** Two daggers +5% crit and +10% crit damage, -10% max health. Two maces 10% stun chance and +10% damage to held enemies, -5% energy regeneration. Two swords 10% extra-swing chance and +5% swing speed, -5% max health.
- **Priest:** Staff +10% heal and shield power and +5% damage, -5% cooldown recovery. Mace and off-hand +10% damage reduction and +10% heal and shield power, -5% damage. Dagger and off-hand +5% crit and +10% damage to afflicted enemies, -2% cooldown recovery.
- **Shaman:** Staff +10% spell area and +5% crit, -5% move speed. Two-hander +5% crit, -10% max health. One-hander and shield +10% heal and shield power and +10% max health, -5% damage.
- **Mage:** Staff +10% spell area and +5% damage, -5% cooldown recovery. Sword and off-hand +10% damage to elites and bosses and +10% crit damage, -5% move speed. Dagger and off-hand +5% crit and +10% damage to held enemies, -5% spell area.
- **Warlock:** Staff +10% spell duration and +10% damage to afflicted enemies, -5% cooldown recovery. Sword and off-hand +5% crit and +10% crit damage, -5% move speed. Dagger and off-hand +10% pet damage and +2% cooldown recovery, -5% damage.
- **Druid:** Mace and off-hand +10% heal and shield power and +2% cooldown recovery, -5% max health. Polearm or two-handed mace +10% damage and +10% max health, -5% move speed. Two-handed staff +10% spell area and +10% spell duration, -5% max health.

**Quality of life**
- The warband screen shows the game's version in the bottom-left corner, under Quit game.
- Mind Flay, Drain Soul and Drain Life no longer drone while they channel; their cast and hit sounds still play.

### Changelog

**Saves**
- Save files are now protected against editing, and saves are checked when the game opens or imports them. A save that fails the checks is corrected, never wiped.
- Your current save carries over as it is.
- Saves exported from v0.5.2 or older can no longer be imported.

## v0.5.0

The Challenge Mode update.

### Patch notes

**Gameplay**
- **Challenge Mode:** clearing Ahn'Qiraj with a class unlocks a Challenge tab. Every zone plays at Ahn'Qiraj's difficulty, plus a 1x-5x challenge slider; a one-time box explains it.
- **Guide:** a new Guide button on the warband screen, with Stats, Enemies, Upgrades and Loot tabs.
- **Warband Standard allies fight like real players now**, and they stay longer.
- **Boss fights last longer:** about 35-55 s for normal bosses and 1.5-2.5 minutes for final bosses, growing from zone to zone. Later bosses in a zone are no longer much tougher than the first.
- **Boss heals are halved.**
- **Some final bosses were reworked** and should feel fairer.
- **Nightmare Suppressors and Black Guard Sentries** are targeted like elites, glow and are elite-sized.
- **Spawns near walls:** standing by a wall or the edge of the map, enemies now come in closer on that side instead of only from the open side.
- **Divine Shield:** its cooldown starts when the shield ends.

**Balance**
- **Talent milestones** at 11 / 21 / 31 points give about +5 % / +10 % / +25 % damage, measured for every starting spell.
- **Redesigned milestones:**
  - Felguard; Force of Nature (Treants); Summon Imp.
  - Tame Beast (a chance on crit, 30 s cooldown).
  - Arcane Concentration (Clearcasting); Rampage (stacking damage).
  - Nightfall (a free Shadow Bolt on ticks); Seed of Corruption (spreading Corruption).
  - Bestial Wrath; Totemic Call; Berserker Rage; Vengeance.
  - Twin Totems (a free Magma Totem on crit).
  - Nature's Grace; Tree of Life; Piercing Shots; Trap Launcher.
  - Bulwark (Defiance); Divine Aegis; Shadow Weaving.
- Many other milestones re-tuned; the tooltips show the numbers.
- **Legendary amulets** give about +30 % damage.
- **Talent experience:**
  - Each zone gives more than the one before.
  - Talent points cost 110, growing 2.4 % a point.
  - About 11 points in Duskwood, 31 after the Scarlet Monastery, 41 after the Sunken Temple, 51 by the end.
- **Enemy health** is re-tuned in every zone; enemies hit as hard as before. **Rares** have less health.

**Quality of life**
- **New boss bar:** a wide health bar with phase marks, a cast bar, and a thin bar for each further boss.
- **Compact action bar:**
  - Gryphons and 8 buttons, the race spell on the 8th.
  - Character, Spellbook, Talents and Options buttons.
  - An XP bar in 10 segments.
- **Glows:**
  - Bosses glow orange; Warsong's elites and rares glow too.
  - The option is now "Glow around elites, rares and bosses".
- **News pulses:** Collection, Talents and the update offer pulse when something is new.
- **Legendary amulet effects:**
  - Every effect has a look, and counting amulets show a counter buff.
  - Stampede sends a herd of beasts.
  - Blink Strike sends a reflection instead of blinking you.
  - Sprint of the Wild brings two spirit cats.

### Changelog

**Fixes**
- Mangle's legendary no longer surges every 2nd Maul to the farthest enemy.
- Warsong bosses show their right names on the boss bar.
- Stampede's beasts are all one size.
- Divine Aegis's tooltip matches its effect.

**Details**
- Challenge Mode rewards are talent experience (as in Ahn'Qiraj x1.1) and normal armour drops. Records, wins, discoveries and codex entries are off in it.

**Install and technical**
- Version 0.5.0; the game offers this update itself on the character screen (from v0.4.1). From v0.4.0 or older, update by hand once with the setup.

## v0.4.1

Updates come to you now: the game can update itself from GitHub.

### Patch notes

**Gameplay**
- **Morbent Fel:** his shield and what breaks it are now much easier to spot.

**Quality of life**
- **Updates in the game:** when a new version is out, the character screen offers it with an Update button that downloads it, checks it and installs it, then restarts the game; your heroes and settings stay. Turn it off in Options > Interface > Check for updates.
- **Smart damage numbers hide more small hits:** every 30 seconds the game takes the average of your hits, each counted as much as it deals, and hides the numbers below 60% of it, so floods of small ticks no longer get through.

### Changelog

**Fixes**
- Item and talent procs, such as the Choker of Enlightenment's extra hits, no longer make your hero play a casting animation; their missiles and hits still show.

**Install and technical**
- The update check asks GitHub's public release list once at start, sends no player data, and stays silent when offline. A download is installed only when it matches the release's checksum file.
- Each release now comes with a `.sha256` checksum file next to the zip.
- From v0.4.0 or older, update by hand once; later versions offer updates in the game.

## v0.4.0

The Stratholme update: Alterac Valley gives way to Stratholme, and the game has a new name.

### Patch notes

**Gameplay**
- **New name:** the game is now called Eternal Survivors.
- **Stratholme replaces Alterac Valley:** hold Slaughter Square with its gates barred against the Scourge. New bosses: Magistrate Barthilas, Baroness Anastari, Nerub'enkan and Maleki the Pallid. Your Alterac Valley progress, gear and looks carry over.
- **Stratholme gear:** its armaments are Stratholme and Scholomance weapons, and its gear has a second look, the **Scholomance sets** (Deathbone Guardian, Bloodmail Regalia, Cadaverous Garb, Necropile Raiment). Every Stratholme piece unlocks both looks.
- **Dire Maul enemies:** the gnolls are gone. Whip Lashers from Dire Maul East and the ghostly Eldreth Sorcerers from the west take their places.

**Balance**
- **Mage, Frost:** the 31-point talent is now Winter's Chill. It no longer summons a Water Elemental, since the Frostbolt starting option already brings one. You still start every run with Frost Nova, casting itself every 9 seconds, and Frost spells deal 9% more. Enemies shatter: +8% damage to slowed, held or fleeing enemies, every Frostbolt hit splashes 5% onto enemies within 5 yd, and Frostbolt deals 20% more.
- **Druid:** Swipe (Cat) is free: no energy cost and no combo point, on its own 3-second cooldown.
- **Cooldown talents:** talents that speed every spell's cooldown give 0.5% a rank, talents for one spell 2% a rank, and talents for two or three spells (or all totems) 1% a rank. Weapon swings keep their pace.

### Changelog

**Stratholme**
- The arena is Slaughter Square (Stratholme), closed by its four gates.
- The Baron's finale follows the dungeon's own order, and spawns resume after it, so Endless keeps going.
- New boss-script pieces: doors a boss script opens, and add waves that come in at a set spot.
- Dungeon arenas now also walk on their terrain beside their buildings.
- The Collection Log and look tooltips can name a gear row; Stratholme's second row reads "Scholomance sets".

**Install and technical**
- The setup finds your existing game folder and moves it to the new name, saves and settings included, then updates it. Your characters' settings move to the new realm name.
- Saves naming Alterac Valley read as Stratholme: wins, gear, looks, the hero's zone and discovered zones carry over.
- The game exe is "Eternal Survivors.exe" and the setup "Eternal Survivors Setup.exe".

## v0.3.0

The boss update: every boss now fights with its own mechanics from WoW, reshaped for a survivors game.

### Patch notes

**Gameplay**
- **Every boss reworked:** all bosses of Elwynn Forest, Duskwood, Scarlet Monastery, Sunken Temple, Dire Maul, Gurubashi Arena, Alterac Valley, Blackrock Mountain and Ahn'Qiraj fight with their own WoW mechanics. Warsong Gulch keeps its player-character bosses.
- **Ground markings like FF14:** every boss area attack is marked on the ground in orange before it lands (circles, rings with a safe middle, cones and lines), with a fill that shows when it hits. Drifting zones and sweeping beams show arrows.
- **Charges run the whole line:** a charging boss runs through its marked line; stand in it and you're hit as it passes.
- **Boss warnings and timer bars:** big warnings with the raid warning sound, and timer bars for each boss's next abilities.
- **Scripted moments:** some bosses bring a moment from their original fight. Which ones, you find out in the run.
- **Things to walk over:** break eggs, cocoons and totems, put out blood fires, and grab relics and crystals by standing on them.
- **Boss voices:** many bosses speak their original lines.
- **Bosses never stun, fear, root or slow you.** The only exceptions are two short scripted cutscenes.

**Balance**
- **Unavoidable boss damage stays the same:** only marked attacks you fail to dodge add damage.
- **Boss sizes:** bosses are now drawn at a consistent size, and final bosses are bigger.
- **Druid:** Blood Frenzy boosts both Swipes, and Swipe (Cat) has its own legendary amulet.
- **Zone health retuned** for the new fights.

**Quality of life**
- **Smart damage numbers:** Options → Interface → Damage numbers. "Smart" hides your smaller hits to declutter the screen, and "All" shows every number. Smart takes the average of your hits over each minute and, for the next minute, hides numbers below 60 % of it. Crits don't count toward the average unless your crit chance is above 50 %.
- **Damage meter names your procs:** talents, rings, trinkets, amulets and legendaries each get their own row instead of "Talent procs".
- **Options → Timers:** a new tab for the boss warnings, the warning sound (untick to mute), the timer bars, the bar handles and resetting their positions.
- **Movable timer bars:** drag them by their handle, and they stay where you put them.
- **Minimap buttons:** one shows or hides the timer bars' drag handles, the other shows or hides the damage meter.
- **New game icon** on the game exe and window.

### Changelog

**Bosses**
- The boss building blocks are marked circles, rings, cones, lines, charges, drifting zones and sweeping beams; lasting zones (fire, poison, plague, webs) with stacking effects; boss scripts on arrival, health, timers and death; add waves; spawn stops and roster swaps; shields and immunity; form changes; enrages; and invisible target units so a dodged spell still shows its impact.
- Warnings: centre-screen text with an icon and the raid warning sound, "soon" pre-warnings, and timer bars that flash under 5 seconds.
- Charges mark a line from the boss through you to 8 yards past you, capped at the ability's length.
- Herod enrages at 25 %, and Angerforge calls his adds at 30 %. Jeklik is silent, and no boss has a cast you need to interrupt.
- Zevrim, the Ironbark Protectors, Lokholar and Korrak use their original models. Jammal'an's and Jin'do's totems are the original troll wards.
- Boss sizes: each model's real size was measured so normal bosses match and final bosses are a third bigger. Nefarian, Azuregos, Eranikus and C'Thun keep their own sizes. Majordomo's Flamewakers and Ragnaros were fixed from far too small.

**Fixes**
- Boss attacks never hit or flinch normal enemies.
- Rogue's Two maces loadout now uses melee maces only, with no caster or healer maces.

**Install and technical**
- The game exe carries the new icon; the setup exe keeps its own.
- The damage meter's proc rows come from each hit's origin. Older clients still read the meter.
