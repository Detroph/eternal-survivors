ESURV - MAKING A BOSS, EXPLAINED
=====================================

This file explains in plain words how to add a boss, and how to give it abilities or take
them away. The technical version is bosses.md in the folder above this one. A ready-to-copy
boss is in docs\game\templates\boss.ron. How to read the data files is explained at the top
of zones.txt.


1. THE SHORT VERSION
--------------------

  1. Open the zone's own file in content\zones (content\zones\elwynn.ron for Elwynn) and find
     its list of bosses ("bosses: [").
  2. Copy the block from docs\game\templates\boss.ron into that list, at the place where the
     boss should arrive (bosses come in order of their spawn_minute).
  3. Give it a name, a model (display_id) and its numbers.
  4. Pick its abilities (section 3).
  5. Exactly one boss per zone is the final one (is_final: true), and it must be last.
  6. Save, then run "cargo test -p esurv-content -p esurv-sim" to check for mistakes.


2. THE BOSS'S NUMBERS
---------------------

    spawn_minute   when it arrives
    move_speed     optional: Some(..) for its own speed; every boss walks at 6 yards
                   per second otherwise (boss_move_speed in enemy_kinds.ron)
    scale          how big it is. Bigger also means it reaches further.
    yell           what it shouts when it arrives
    aggro_sound    optional: Some(..), the original boss's own voice line (a SoundEntries
                   number) heard as it arrives; the words shown stay the yell (bosses.md,
                   section 5, lists them)

  A boss has no health or damage of its own in the file. The game works them out when it
  arrives, from the zone's difficulty and the minute (see zones.txt, section 6): a boss gets
  24 times a normal enemy's health and twice its damage, the final boss 28 times the health
  and the same damage. It swings every 2 seconds, and all its abilities are worked out from
  that damage.

  Below 30% health a boss gets ANGRY for the rest of the fight: it moves faster, hits 40%
  harder and uses its abilities 30% more often.


3. ABILITIES - ADDING, CHANGING, REMOVING
-----------------------------------------

  The abilities list looks like this:

      abilities: [
          Charge(look_spell: 100),
          Summon(),
      ],

  Each line is one ability: its kind and what it looks like. Its numbers (how often, how big,
  how hard) are the same for every boss and are written ONCE, in content\enemy_kinds.ron
  (the "boss_abilities" part). Change a number there and every boss with that ability follows.
  "cooldown" there is the seconds between uses; the first use comes a bit earlier (after 60%
  of the cooldown).

    - To REMOVE an ability: delete its whole line.
    - To ADD one: copy a line from the list below into the brackets.
    - A boss with no abilities at all is fine too:  abilities: [],
    - To give ONE boss its own number, add it to its line, for example
          Stomp(look_spell: 6343, radius: Some(12.0), cooldown: Some(10.0)),
      Every number the kind has can be set this way (cooldown, radius, delay, damage_mult,
      count).

  The five kinds (copy the line, then pick the looks):

    Charge(look_spell: 100),
        Rushes to where you stand and hits everything near that spot for double damage.
        Every 8 seconds.

    Stomp(look_spell: 6343),
        Hits you for double damage if you are within 10 yards of the boss. Every 8 seconds.

    GroundBurst(telegraph_spell: 10, impact_spell: 122),
        Marks the ground where you stand. 2 seconds later it explodes there for three times
        the boss's damage. Walk out of the circle in time! Every 6 seconds.

    Volley(look_spell: 133),
        Fires 6 missiles of that WoW spell: one where you stand, the others close around you.
        Each one hurts a little (half its normal damage). Every 6 seconds.

    Summon(),
        the boss shouts for help and calls helpers in a ring around the boss: 6, 7, 8, 10 or 12
        for a zone's first to fifth boss. They are the first enemies of the zone's list.
        Every 15 seconds.

  The look_spell numbers are WoW spell numbers: they decide what you see and hear
  (100 = Charge, 6343 = Thunder Clap, 133 = Fireball, 122 = Frost Nova).


4. A COMPLETELY NEW KIND OF ABILITY
-----------------------------------

  The five kinds can be mixed freely, and their numbers tuned in enemy_kinds.ron. Something
  that works differently (pulling you in, a shield, a beam ...) needs a small piece of code
  first; bosses.md explains where.
  After that it can be used in any zone file like the others.

5. THE BUILDING BLOCKS (MARKS, ZONES, OBJECTS, SCRIPTS, WARNINGS)
-----------------------------------------------------------------

  The rules: every boss attack that covers an area is MARKED on the ground first (a purple
  Shadow Fissure marking that fills up), long enough to walk out of it (1 to 2.5 seconds). Boss attacks only
  ever hit YOU, never the other enemies. Bosses never stun, fear, root or slow you. bosses.md,
  section 6, has every field; here is what each block is for.

    Marked(shape: Circle(radius: 10.0), at: Boss, impact_spell: 6304),
        A marked attack. Shapes: Circle(radius), Ring(inner, outer) (safe in the middle),
        Cone(length, angle), Line(length, width). at: Boss (round the boss; it stands still
        while it winds up), Hero (where you stand), NearHero(count: 3, spread: 7.0) (several
        round you). Optional: wind_up, damage_mult, cooldown, leaves (a lasting zone where it
        lands), leaves_object (an egg or cocoon where it lands), missile: true (a slow bolt).

    Rush(look_spell: 100),
        A marked line from the boss to you; then the boss runs along it. fade_spell makes it
        fade into shadow while it waits (Lupos).

    Lay(object: (name: "Egg Sac", display_id: 6171, glow_spell: 20922, hatch: Some((after: 10.0,
        enemy: "Mine Spider", count: 3))), near: Boss(5.0)),
        Puts objects on the ground. YOU BREAK OR TAKE AN OBJECT BY STANDING ON IT for half a
        second (brushing past does not count); its gold glow fills while you stand there.
        Attacks never hit objects. Left alone, an egg hatches.

    A lasting zone: (look_spell: 28158, radius: 3.5, seconds: 8.0, damage_mult: 0.0,
        affliction: Some((look_spell: 744, damage_mult: 0.05, every: 1.0, seconds: 6.0,
        max_stacks: 5)))
        Stays on the ground and hurts you while you stand in it; an affliction is a stacking
        damage effect it leaves on you. drift: 2.0 makes it creep towards you; follows: true
        keeps it on its boss (an aura you must stay out of).

  A SCRIPT is what a boss does at a health share, on a timer, as it arrives or as it dies:

      script: [
          (
              on: Health(50.0),
              warn: Some((text: "Lapdogs! Adds incoming!", colour: Orange)),
              soon: Some("Lapdogs soon"),
              run: [
                  Yell(text: "Lapdogs, all of you!", sound: Some(5782)),
                  Wave(enemy: "Defias Blackguard", count: 6, at: Ring(8.0)),
              ],
          ),
      ],

  on: Arrival, Health(50.0), Every(20.0), After(10.0) or Death. Things a script can do: Yell
  (with the original voice: then the words must be what the voice says), Emote, Wave (adds,
  in a ring or from the edges), StopSpawns / ResumeSpawns, Roster(only: Undead) (from then on
  only that kind spawns), RaiseDead (Morbent Fel), Shield and Mortal (a shield that drops for a
  while), Place (an object), LayZone, Kit (a new set of abilities: a new phase), Weapons, Look
  (a new model), Kneel, Enrage, and Pause (a short cutscene, at most 5 seconds, where
  everything freezes). Adds that only a boss brings are roster enemies with only_summoned: true.

  More things a script can do (the Scarlet Monastery to Zul'Gurub bosses use them): Later
  (seconds: 3.0, run: [...]) runs things a little later, even inside a Pause; On(boss: "...",
  run: [...]) acts as another boss; Call(boss: "...") brings in a boss marked called: true
  (Whitemane, who only comes when Mograine falls); Revive(boss: "...", health: 0.5, ...) brings a
  fallen boss back where it fell; Unshield; Heal(on: Near(15.0) / Myself / Boss("..."),
  share: 0.05, look_spell: ..., beam_spell: ...) heals the boss's side, from the boss, from adds
  named in from: Some("..."), or from a totem; Cast(ability: Marked(...)) casts a marked attack at
  once; ClearZones, Wear, Speed, Bolster (makes adds or the boss stronger), and Siphon (Hakkar's
  Blood Siphon). on: AddDied with add: Some("...") runs each time that add dies, at its spot;
  on: HeroFell each time you lose a life to the boss. until_health / from_health limit an Every
  line to a phase. A wave can come at: Sides(4): four packs from four sides, their lanes marked
  first; or at: Spot(x: ..., y: ..., spread: 5.0): round a spot of the map (Stratholme's undead
  coming in at the square's gate). Door(name: "...", open: true) swings one of the zone's doors
  open (false: shut again). An object can have pulse (a totem that acts every few seconds), left_alone (something
  bad happens unless you stand on it in time) and on_all_taken (when the last one is taken).
  A marked attack with from: Some("...") is laid from each of those adds (Immol'thar's Eyes).
  swing_mult: Some(0.0) makes a boss that never swings (a caster).

  More things a script can do (Stratholme, Blackrock Mountain and Ahn'Qiraj use them):
    Waves(waves: [(run: [Wave(...)], warn: ...), ...], gap: 20.0, immune: true, aloof: true,
        bar: Some("Next wave"), counter: Some("Qiraji left"), then: [Yell(...)])
        A run of waves: each comes once the last one is dead (or after gap seconds). immune: the
        boss takes no damage meanwhile; aloof: it stands apart doing nothing. A timer bar counts
        to the next wave and a counter shows the adds left. then: what happens once they are all
        done (Rajaxx joins, Rend mounts Gyth). Majordomo's eight guards are one such wave.
    Gather(within: 40.0, ahead: 6.0, look_spell: 21147)
        Pulls every enemy near the boss into one clump in front of it (never you).
    SpellImmune(seconds: 10.0, look_spell: 22067)
        For a while spells do nothing to the boss; weapon swings and pets still do.
    Empower(look_spell: 29131, faster: 0.1, harder: 0.1, max_stacks: 5, seconds: 0.0)
        A stack of rage: its marked attacks come sooner and hit harder, and it runs faster
        (never its swing). seconds: 8.0 makes it wear off (a frenzy).
    Mend(by: "Anvilrage Medic", share: 0.01, look_spell: 15586)
        Each living add of that name heals the boss: kill them.
    ClassCall(seconds: 10.0, calls: [(class: "warrior", weakens: 0.5, look_spell: 23397,
        warn: (text: ...)), ...])
        Twists your class for a while: you deal less damage (never a stun), or run: brings adds.
    RaiseFallen(rise_as: "Bone Construct", look_spell: 17473, most: 16)
        Where the boss's wave adds fell, these rise.
    Cameo(enemy: "Ragnaros", at: Ring(0.0), delay: 4.0, seconds: 8.0, run: [Yell(...)],
        abilities: [Marked(...)])
        A figure rises for a moment, takes no damage, never moves, does its kit, then sinks.
  A Marked attack may also have: at: Across(count, spread) (lines across the ground),
  chain: Some(0.5) (each further mark lands a little later), brings: Some((enemy: "Eye
  Tentacle", count: 1, rooted: true)) (adds burst from where it lands; rooted ones stay there),
  sweep: Some((turn: 25.0, seconds: 8.0, damage_mult: 0.5, look_spell: 20924)) (a line that stays
  and sweeps round the boss: a beam).

  WARNINGS: warn: Some((text: ..., colour: Red)) on an ability, a script line or an object shows
  the text big across the screen with its icon and the raid warning sound, and puts a timer bar
  at the left that counts down to the next time (a bar moves up and flashes in its last 5
  seconds). soon: on a health line warns 5 points before it. Colours: Red (move out), Orange
  (adds), Yellow (do something), Purple (a phase), Green (poison), Blue. Options > Interface
  turns the bars, the warnings and their sound on or off.

  DAMAGE: a reworked boss must take from you, whatever you do, as much as it did before (its
  swings once it catches you, its unmarked hits). swing_mult: Some(1.5) makes its swing carry
  what its old unmarked abilities did. The marked attacks, zones and eggs only hurt a hero who
  does not get out of the way, on top of that.
