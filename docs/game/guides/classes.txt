ESURV - CLASSES, SPELLS AND TALENTS, EXPLAINED
===================================================

This file explains in plain words how heroes and their spells work. The technical version is
classes.md in the folder above this one. How to read the data files (quotes, commas, brackets)
is explained at the top of zones.txt.


1. WHERE IT LIVES
-----------------

    content\classes.ron     the 9 classes
    content\abilities.ron   every spell (102 of them)
    content\talents.ron     the talent trees (3 per class, 13 talents each)


2. A CLASS
----------

    id: "warrior"                   internal name
    name: Class(1)                  shown to the player: WoW's own class name, read from your
                                    WoW files by its number (a name of our own would go in
                                    quotes, name: "Spellblade"). Talents, talent trees, races
                                    and their passives do the same: Spell(...), TalentTab(...),
                                    Race(...) where WoW has the name, quotes where it does not.
    wow_class_id: 1                 WoW's own class number; the game shows the class icon,
                                    name colour and so on from it
    power: Rage                     rage, energy or mana bar (shown, not used in play yet)
    description                     the lines the class picker shows about it
    color, icon                     kept in the file but not read yet: the menus use the
                                    stock colour and icon of wow_class_id
    base_health: 240.0              health at level 1
    base_damage_reduction: 0.2      how much damage the class ignores: 0.2 = 20%
                                    (plate 20%, mail and leather 15%, cloth 8%; never more
                                    than 60% total)
    base_lifesteal: 0.02            every hit heals you for this share of the damage it
                                    dealt: 0.02 = 2% (rogue 3%; paladin, priest, warlock 1%;
                                    the others 0); talents,
                                    rings and amulets can add more
    auto_attack: Some((damage: 28.0, swing: 1.6, range: 6.0))
                                    a basic weapon swing (only warrior, paladin and rogue have
                                    one); swing = seconds between hits. None = no swing.
    abilities: [...]                the 10 spells this class can be offered when levelling up
    starting_ability: "cleave"      the spell every run starts with
    spell_damage_scale: 1.0         multiplies the damage (and heals, shields) of this class's
                                    spells; never their cooldowns or durations, never the race
                                    spell. 1 if left out.


3. HOW STRONG THE HERO IS
-------------------------

  Every level up:
      +9 max health, +3% damage, +0.15 health per second, and a free heal of 20% health.

  Starting values for everyone:
      5% chance to crit, crits do double damage, 1 health per second,
      runs at 7 yards per second, picks up loot from 9 yards away.

  Bonuses from race, talents, gear, rings, trinkets and necklaces are added on top.
  Some limits:
      - damage taken can be reduced by at most 60%
      - you can be at most 2.2 times faster than normal
      - crit chance above 100% turns into extra crit DAMAGE instead

  Experience needed for the next level (it grows each level):
      200 + 65 x (level - 1) to the power 1.5, rounded, but never more than 20000
      level 1->2: 200     2->3: 265     10->11: 1955     40->41: 16031     46 and up: 20000
  Without experience bonuses you reach about level 40 by minute 16 in Elwynn; level 60
  needs the Human racial and an experience trinket. The numbers (200, 65, the power 1.5
  and the cap 20000) are in content\balance.ron, under "experience".
  Experience left over after a level-up counts toward the next one. Highest level: 99.

  Experience from kills (also in content\balance.ron):
      normal enemy   100 at normal density; more density means less each (50 at 200%)
      elite          1000
      boss           2400, then 800 more for each boss already killed (2400, 3200, 4000 ...)
      rare       straight to the next level
  Orbs look small below 200, bigger below 1000, and big and red from 1000.


3a. RACES
---------

  Your hero is a real character of its race: you see that race's body, and it belongs to
  that race's faction (Alliance or Horde). Every race brings two things:

    - PASSIVES: small lasting bonuses (more health, more gold, faster cooldowns ...).
    - A RACE SPELL: it casts by itself, like your other spells, but it never shows up on
      level-up cards.

      Human      Perception (every 30 s, +15% damage for 10 s)
                 +10% experience, +20% gold
      Orc        Blood Fury (every 40 s, +35% damage for 12 s)
                 +10% max health
      Dwarf      Stoneform (every 45 s, a shield worth 30% of your health)
                 +5% damage, +30% pickup range
      Night Elf  Shadowmeld (when nearly dead, 3 s where nothing hurts you; once per 75 s)
                 +10% movement speed, take 5% less damage
      Undead     Will of the Forsaken (when nearly dead, 4 s of immunity; once per 60 s)
                 +1 health per kill, take 5% less damage
      Tauren     War Stomp (every 15 s, stuns enemies around you for 2 s)
                 +15% max health
      Gnome      Escape Artist (every 20 s, roots enemies around you for 2 s)
                 cooldowns recover 10% faster, +20% luck
      Troll      Berserking (every 35 s, cooldowns recover 30% faster for 10 s)
                 +2 health per second, +3% critical strike chance

  These numbers are exactly what happens in play: Perception really comes every 30 seconds.
  (The class's spell_damage_scale never touches them. Faster cooldowns from talents, gear or
  the Gnome passive still speed race spells up too.)

  Races live in content\races.ron. To change one, edit its passives (same stat names as
  talents) or point "spell" at another spell from abilities.ron.
  During a run the spellbook (P) shows each passive as WoW's own racial trait of the same
  name. Where our name differs from WoW's, the line stock_spell: Some(Spell(...)) points at
  WoW's spell by its number (the Dwarf's "Treasure Finding" shows as its stock racial).


3b. EXTRA RACE AND CLASS PAIRS
------------------------------

  Besides everything WoW 1.12 lets you make, six more heroes
  can be made on the create screen:

      Dwarf Shaman     Human Hunter     Gnome Priest
      Orc Mage         Troll Warlock    Undead Paladin

  They are ordinary heroes: the class plays exactly as it always does (same spells, pets,
  totems, talents and gear) and the race gives its usual passives and race spell. Nothing
  about them has numbers of its own.

  WoW has no starting clothes for these pairs, so each wears the class's starting clothes of
  another race: one of its own side (Alliance or Horde) if any has them, else of the other
  side, the first in WoW's race order. So the Dwarf Shaman wears the Orc Shaman's clothes,
  the Human Hunter the Dwarf Hunter's, the Gnome Priest the Human Priest's, the Orc Mage the
  Undead Mage's, the Troll Warlock the Orc Warlock's and the Undead Paladin the Human
  Paladin's.

  A Dwarf Shaman's totem is the mace Ironfoe, planted in the ground by its grip with its head
  up, as tall as an ordinary totem. The ordinary totem's body is gone, but its magic stays:
  its glows swirl around the mace's shaft and its elemental flame burns just over the mace's
  head. The mace stands still; the magic moves as the totem's always does. The game builds
  that look while it runs, from your own WoW files (the totem and Ironfoe's model and
  texture); other races' totems look as always.

  In content\races.ron, each race lists the classes it adds under "added_classes", and the
  dwarf's "totem_look" names the planted model ("part": Weapon(23618) is Ironfoe), how big it
  is ("scale"), how it is tipped up and turned ("pitch_degrees", "turn_degrees"), how deep it
  goes into the ground ("sink", in yards), how far the totem's flame is lifted ("raise_effects",
  in yards) and whether the ordinary totem's body is hidden ("hide_totem_body"). To let a race
  be one more class, add the class's id to its list.


4. A SPELL
----------

    id, name, icon                  names and the icon picture
    look_spell: 133                 which WoW spell effect plays (133 = Fireball)
    school: Fire                    Physical, Holy, Fire, Nature, Frost, Shadow or Arcane
    delivery: Projectile            HOW the spell reaches enemies (see the list below)
    damage: 72.0                    damage per hit
    cooldown: 1.8                   seconds between casts - spells cast BY THEMSELVES when ready
    range: 30.0                     how far it reaches (0 = around you)
    radius: 0.0                     size of an area or nova
    targets: 1                      how many enemies one cast hits
    duration / ticks                for things that last: how long, and how many hits
    effect: None                    an extra effect: slow, root, stun, fear, heal, shield, ...

  THE 11 WAYS A SPELL CAN WORK (the "delivery" kind):

    Projectile        shoots at the nearest enemies (Fireball, Frostbolt, Auto Shot)
    Chain             hits random nearby enemies one after another (Holy Shock, Arcane Missiles)
    Nova              hits everything around you (Whirlwind, Frost Nova, Holy Nova)
    GroundArea        a lasting area that keeps hurting (Consecration, Blizzard, traps)
                      - placed on you, or on the biggest group of enemies in range
    Bounce            a chain that jumps from enemy to enemy (only Chain Lightning)
    Melee             the closest enemies within reach (Cleave, Sinister Strike)
    DamageOverTime    a poison/curse/bleed that keeps hurting (Rend, Corruption, Serpent Sting)
    Finisher          a melee hit that does DOUBLE damage to enemies under 30% health
                      (Execute, Eviscerate, Backstab)
    Cone              a cone in front of you (Mind Flay, Cone of Cold)
    SelfEffect        heal, shield, buff or immunity on yourself:
                        - heals only fire when you are under 85% health
                        - a shield only fires when you have no shield
                        - immunity only fires when you are under 35% health
                        - buffs only fire when an enemy is within 30 yards
    Totem             puts down a totem when an enemy is within 25 yards

  HOW MUCH DAMAGE ONE HIT DOES:

      your damage bonus x the spell's damage x the class's spell_damage_scale
        x (bonus from spell cards)
      then: maybe a critical hit (x2 or more), then bonuses against stunned/slowed enemies,
      enemies under 30% health, or enemies you already poisoned/cursed.

  HOW HARD EACH KIND OF SPELL HITS:

      Every damaging class spell has a role, and its damage is set so it deals this much
      at level 1 with no upgrades (full table: docs\game\spells.md, section 5c):
        filler (one target, cooldown up to 2.5 s)   40 damage a second
        nuke (one target)          60 a second at a 3 s cooldown, up to 70 at 7.5 s or more
        few targets (2-3)          45 a second in total
        bouncing chain             50 a second over the whole chain
        random hits                60 a second in total
        finisher                   40 a second (45 for the 2-target ones), x2 under 30% health
        over time                  100 damage to each enemy for every 4 s it lasts
        starting spells, areas, stuns/roots/slows/fear, buffs, heals, shields, totems:
                                   unchanged
        fixed                      a damage a second set by hand for one spell:
                                   Mortal Strike 61, Bloodthirst 40, Exorcism 62.25,
                                   Arcane Shot 50, Aimed Shot 64.4, Ghostly Strike 30,
                                   Hammer of Wrath 40, Judgement 67.75, Mind Blast 63.3,
                                   Holy Fire about 63 (its hit and its burn together),
                                   Devouring Plague 92.4 over a pack (66% of Shadow
                                   Word: Pain's 140)
      The test in crates\esurv-sim\tests\spell_roles.rs checks every class spell.

      WARRIOR COOLDOWNS: Mortal Strike, Bloodthirst and Shield
      Slam every 6 s, Execute every 3.5 s, Rend every 3 s, Whirlwind every 8 s, Thunder
      Clap every 7 s, Battle Shout every 25 s (+30% damage for 12.5 s); the damage of each
      was worked out again so it keeps its damage a second (docs\game\spells.md, 5c).

      PALADIN COOLDOWNS: Consecration every 13.5 s for 8 s,
      Exorcism 8 s, Hammer of Wrath 4 s (double on enemies under 30% health), Judgement 6
      s, Holy Shock 8 s (heals 10% of max health per hit), Holy Wrath 10 s, Hammer of
      Justice 10 s (3 s stun), Holy Light 16.5 s (heals 35% once you are at 65% health or
      less), Divine Shield 60 s (6 s immunity), Holy Shield 20 s (lasts 10 s); damage
      worked out again the same way.

      HUNTER COOLDOWNS: Arcane Shot 3 s (50 a second), Multi-Shot
      6 s, Aimed Shot 10 s, Serpent Sting 3 s, Concussive Shot 8 s (4 s slow), Explosive
      Trap 8 s, Immolation Trap 12 s lasting 8 s, Volley 13.5 s lasting 8 s (like Consecration), Frost
      Trap 10 s (1.5 s root); Auto Shot unchanged; the rest keep their damage a second.

      ROGUE COOLDOWNS: Eviscerate 6 s, Backstab 4 s, Ghostly
      Strike 4 s (30 a second, shield kept: a defensive strike), Rupture 6 s lasting 6 s (300 to each of 2: 50 a second on one),
      Blade Flurry 4 s, Kidney Shot 8 s (2.5 s stun), Gouge 6 s, Slice and Dice 20 s
      lasting 10 s, Adrenaline Rush 30 s lasting 12 s; Sinister Strike unchanged; the rest
      keep their damage a second.

      PRIEST COOLDOWNS: Mind Blast 6 s, Holy Fire 7 s (a hit of
      280 and a burn of 160 over 5 s on the same enemy), Shadow Word: Pain 3 s, Devouring
      Plague 6 s (185 to each of 3 over 8.4 s), Holy Nova 6 s, Mind Flay 4 s, Psychic
      Scream 15 s, Power Word: Shield 15 s (absorbs 25% of max health; cast again only
      once the shield is broken or gone, its cooldown counted from the cast), Renew 15 s
      (heals 30% of max health over 10 s); Smite unchanged; the rest keep their damage a
      second.

      SHAMAN COOLDOWNS: Lightning Bolt 2 s, Chain Lightning 4 s,
      Earth Shock 4 s, Frost Shock 4 s, Flame Shock 4 s lasting 8 s, Fire Nova 7 s, Magma
      Totem 13.5 s lasting 8 s (5 a second to each enemy, half of Consecration), Searing
      Totem 15 s lasting 10 s, Lightning Shield 20 s lasting 20 s, Healing Wave 12.5 s
      (heals 26.5% at 85% health or less); the rest keep their damage a second.

      MAGE COOLDOWNS: Fireball 2 s (80), Fire Blast 4 s (240),
      Pyroblast 10 s (700), Arcane Missiles 4 s (4 missiles of 60), Frost Nova 10 s, Blast
      Wave 12 s, Cone of Cold 6 s, Blizzard 12 s lasting 8 s in 8 ticks (140 to each
      enemy), Flamestrike 10 s lasting 8 s (46 to each enemy inside as it lands, then 70
      over 4 ticks), Ice Barrier 20 s absorbing 35% of max health (cast again only once
      the shield is broken or gone); Frostbolt and Arcane Explosion unchanged; the rest
      keep their damage a second.

      WARLOCK COOLDOWNS: Searing Pain 2.5 s (150), Corruption
      2 s on 1 enemy (220 over 8 s in 8 ticks), Curse of Agony 5 s on 1 enemy (600 over
      10 s in 10 ticks: 60 a second), Immolate 6 s (600 over 12 s in 12 ticks: 50 a second), Drain Life 6 s (200
      over 4 s in 4 ticks, still healing 60% of each tick), Rain of Fire 12 s lasting 8 s
      in 4 ticks (102 to each enemy), Hellfire 6 s (3 novas 1 s apart, 13.5 to each enemy
      around you each time), Death Coil 9 s (225, healing 50% of what it deals), Fear 8 s
      (7.5 to each of 3, fear 4 s); Shadow Bolt unchanged.

      DRUID COOLDOWNS: Starfire 6 s, Hurricane 15 s lasting 10 s,
      Swipe 3 s, Entangling Roots 8 s (3 s root), Rejuvenation 15 s (heals 35% over 10 s),
      Regrowth 12.5 s (heals 26.5%, like Healing Wave), Thorns 30 s lasting 30 s, Moonfire
      115 and Insect Swarm 33 to each enemy (150 and 33 a second over a pack); Wrath unchanged; the rest keep their
      damage a second.

      A NOVA THAT REPEATS AROUND YOU: a nova with 2 or more
      `ticks` (Hellfire) goes off that many times per cast, `duration` / `ticks` seconds
      apart, the first at once. Each one bursts around where you stand at that moment, so
      it follows you. Each grows with the spell's damage; longer durations add whole novas
      at the same spacing. Only the first can use a Bloodthirst charge, and the rest stop
      if you fall.

      A HIT THAT LEAVES A BURN: a spell with `burn` (Holy Fire)
      also burns every enemy its hit lands on: an effect over time of its own that starts
      when the hit lands, grows with the hit's damage, gets whole extra ticks from longer
      durations, and renews like the spell's other effects over time.

      A GROUND AREA THAT LANDS WITH AN IMPACT: a ground area
      with `impact` (Flamestrike) strikes every enemy inside it at once as it lands, then
      ticks as usual. The impact grows with the spell's damage, but not with its duration.

      BLOODTHIRST: when it hits, you get 5 healing charges
      for 8 s. Each of your next weapon swings or spell casts that hits uses one and
      heals 4% of your max health (one per cast, however many enemies it hits; ticks,
      lasting areas and totems use none). Hitting again starts it over; leftovers
      vanish after 8 s. The buff frame shows the charges left as stacks.


5. LEVEL-UP CARDS
-----------------

  Each level you pick 1 of 3 cards: a NEW spell (you can hold at most 6) or an UPGRADE to a
  spell you know.

  HOW THE 3 CARDS ARE CHOSEN
      Every spell of your class you don't know yet is a possible "new spell" card, and every
      spell you know that can still get better is a possible "upgrade" card. The game draws 3
      different ones. New spells are more likely while you know fewer than 3 spells; after
      that, upgrades become a bit more likely than new spells. If there is nothing at all to
      offer, you get 25 gold instead.

  A NEW SPELL card with a colour starts the spell stronger:

        colour      spells that do damage      other spells (buffs, shields...)
        white       nothing extra              nothing extra
        green       +15% damage                -7% cooldown
        blue        +25% damage                -10% cooldown
        purple      +34% damage                -13% cooldown
        orange      +46% damage                -17% cooldown

  Chances at the start of a run: white 60%, green 25%, blue 10%, purple 4%, orange 1%.
  Luck and a higher level make the better colours more likely.

  Rerolling the 3 cards: 3 times free, then 100 gold, 300, 900, 2700... (x3 each time).

  WHAT AN UPGRADE CARD DOES
      It improves ONE thing about the spell (TWO on an orange card), picked at random from what
      the spell can still improve:
          power      more damage (or healing). Each power upgrade adds less than the last.
          cooldown   casts more often, down to at most -80%
          targets    hits more enemies, up to 12 extra
          area       bigger area, up to 5 times the size
          duration   lasts longer, up to 6 times as long
      Which of these a spell can ever get is listed in its "upgrades" in abilities.ron
      (Power, Cooldown, Targets, Area, Duration).
      Better colours make each step bigger. Every upgrade also adds a little power (+4% at
      first, a bit less at higher ranks).

  THERE IS NO HIGHEST RANK. A spell keeps getting upgrade cards as long as something about it
  can still improve noticeably (at least 0.5%). Once everything is at its limit, it stops
  showing up.


6. TALENTS
----------

  Each class has 3 trees with 13 talents each, in rows from top (row 0) to the bottom
  capstone (row 6). A talent can:
    - add to a hero stat each rank ("+2% cooldown recovery per rank")
    - improve spells each rank (more damage, shorter cooldown, more targets, bigger area, or
      longer duration): one spell, a few named spells, every spell of a school ("Holy
      spells: +2% damage"), or every spell of one delivery ("Effects over time: +10%").
      Talents and the amulet together can cut a spell's cooldown by at most 60%.
    - be a MILESTONE that changes how things work. There are about 20 kinds, for example:
        * start every run with a spell
        * cast a spell by itself every few seconds (Bladestorm: a free Whirlwind every 3 s)
        * every Nth kill or cast gives a free spell (Hot Streak, Rampage)
        * a chance to cast a free spell when something happens (when you crit, when hit)
        * your hits hit extra enemies, hit twice, splash around, stun, root, or add a bleed
        * enemies you kill explode
        * cheat death: survive a killing blow once in a while
        * a pet fights beside you (wolf, raptor, imp, treants, felguard)
      The pets are listed in content\pets.ron (model, damage, attack speed).
      The mage's Water Elemental comes from the Frostbolt starting option, not a talent.
      The Frost capstone, Winter's Chill, gives Shatter instead of a
      second elemental: Frost Nova casting itself every 9 s, +8% damage to slowed,
      held or fleeing enemies, every Frostbolt hit splashing 5% onto enemies within 5 yd of its
      target, Frost spells +9%, Frostbolt +20%. Base (v0.3.0): a Water Elemental pet, the same
      Frost Nova, Frost spells +9%, Frostbolt +90%.
      In talents.ron each milestone is written out with names, for example
          FreeCast(chance: 0.2, cooldown: 1.5, spell: "thunder_clap",
                   trigger: StruckInMelee, filter: Any)
      reads: "20% chance, at most every 1.5 s, to cast a free Thunder Clap when an enemy
      hits you in melee, whatever hit you". The "trigger" is WHEN it happens (Hit, Crit,
      Kill, Cast, StruckInMelee ...) and the "filter" is WHICH spells count (Any, one spell,
      a list of spells, a school like Fire, a kind of spell like DamageOverTime, your weapon
      swings, or your pets). The full list
      is in classes.md. How some of them behave in detail is still being checked.

  Talent points come from TALENT EXPERIENCE earned at the end of every run:
      10 per minute survived
      + a little per kill (0.05 each, less at high density)
      + 40 per boss killed
      + 250 for winning
      then x the square root of the zone's difficulty: x1.0 in Elwynn, x1.41 in Duskwood
      ... x3.16 in Ahn'Qiraj (harder zone = more)
      then up to x2 more for harder Run Options.

  Each talent point costs 2.5% more than the last: 145, 149, 152, ... 498 for the 51st
  (145 x 1.025 to the power of the point number - 1). A class can earn at most 51 points.
  Winning each zone once, in order, gives 11 points after
  Duskwood, the 21st during Arathi, the 31st during the zone at difficulty 6 (the Arena
  until there is one) and all 51 after Blackrock once that zone is in. The numbers live in
  crates\esurv-sim\src\game\rewards.rs and content\balance.ron (zone_scaling).


7. CHANGING OR ADDING
---------------------

  - Change a number: edit it in the .ron file and restart the game.
  - New spell: copy a spell block in abilities.ron, give it a new id, choose how it works
    (the delivery kind above) and a WoW spell effect, then add its id to a class's list.
  - A class: the game already uses all nine classes WoW 1.12 knows, and tells them apart by
    wow_class_id. So you reshape one of them: keep its id and wow_class_id, change the rest,
    its 3 talent trees and its gear set. A tenth class needs code. A new class spell also
    needs its role in the spell roles test (see AUTHORING.md, sections 7 and 8).
  - A completely new KIND of spell (or a new talent rule) needs a small piece of code first;
    after that, data files can use it. Your planned ideas (rage, procs like Hot Streak) will
    plug in here.
  - Check your changes: run "cargo test -p esurv-content -p esurv-sim".
  - Worked examples of every kind of change: AUTHORING.md.
