ESURV - MAKING A SPELL, EXPLAINED
======================================

This file explains in plain words how to add a new spell. The technical version is spells.md
in the folder above this one. A ready-to-copy spell is in docs\game\templates\spell.ron.
How to read the data files (quotes, commas, brackets) is explained at the top of zones.txt.


1. THE SHORT VERSION
--------------------

  1. Open content\abilities.ron.
  2. Copy the block from docs\game\templates\spell.ron into the list.
  3. Give it a new id (lowercase, no spaces, e.g. "frost_comet") and a name.
  4. Pick how it looks (look_spell and icon, section 3).
  5. Pick how it works (delivery, section 4) and set its numbers.
     Then say what level-up cards may improve (section 6) and what kind of spell it is
     (section 7).
  6. Open content\classes.ron and add the new id to a class's list of abilities.
  7. Save, then run "cargo test -p esurv-content -p esurv-sim" to check for mistakes.


2. EVERY LINE OF A SPELL
------------------------

    id: "my_new_spell"         the spell's internal name. Classes and talents use it.
    name: "My New Spell"       what the player sees. If WoW already has a spell of exactly
                               that name, write its number instead, name: Spell(133),
                               and the game reads the name from your own WoW files (any
                               rank of the spell will do). A name of our own stays in
                               quotes. Inside a description, {Spell(133)} shows WoW's
                               name for that spell.
    icon: "Spell_Fire_FlameBolt"
                               the icon picture, by its name in the WoW client.
    look_spell: 133            which real WoW spell it looks like (133 = Fireball). This
                               also decides how fast its missile flies (section 3).
    description: "Hurls a bolt at {targets:the nearest enemy|the # nearest enemies} within
                 {range} yd for {damage} {school} damage."
                               what a card says the first time it offers the spell, in your
                               own words. The words in curly brackets are filled in with the
                               spell's real numbers when the card shows: {damage}, {range},
                               {targets}, {radius}, {duration}, {school} and more (the full
                               list is in spells.md, section 2a). {targets:one|# many} picks
                               the words by the number (# is the number). The game will not
                               start with a name it does not know, and the tests check that
                               every spell has each number its text asks for.
    school: Fire               Physical, Holy, Fire, Nature, Frost, Shadow or Arcane.
    delivery: Projectile       HOW it reaches enemies (section 4).
    categories: [DirectDamage, Ranged]
                               what kind of spell it is (section 7).
    tags: [Nuke]               what the spell is about (section 7a).
    upgrades: [Power, Cooldown, Targets]
                               what level-up cards can improve (section 6).
    damage: 40.0               damage per hit. For lasting areas, effects over time and
                               totems: the damage over the whole duration, shared out over
                               the ticks. 0 if it does no damage.
    cooldown: 2.0              time between casts. Spells cast by themselves. In the game
                               it is exactly this (then made shorter by upgrades, talents
                               and gear), and never goes under 0.25 s. Damage is multiplied
                               by the class's spell_damage_scale (classes.ron, 1 for now);
                               cooldowns and durations never are.
    range: 30.0                how far it reaches. 0 = around you.
    radius: 0.0                size of a nova, area, cone or totem. The spell's look is
                               grown or shrunk in the game to match it.
    targets: 1                 how many enemies one cast hits.
    duration: 0.0              how long areas, effects over time, buffs and totems last.
    ticks: 0                   how many hits happen during that duration. A Nova with ticks
                               2 or more goes off that many times per cast,
                               duration / ticks seconds apart, the first at once, each
                               around where you then stand, each for the full damage
                               (Hellfire: duration 3.0, ticks 3 = at 0, 1 and 2 seconds).
                               Longer durations add whole novas.
    pulse_spell: Some(8187)    optional, for totems: the WoW spell each pulse shows
                               (leave it out to use look_spell).
    effect: None               an extra effect (section 5).
    burn: Some((damage: 160.0, duration: 5.0, ticks: 5))
                               optional: every enemy the hit lands on also burns, here for
                               160 in all, in 5 even ticks over 5 seconds, starting when
                               the hit lands. It grows with the hit's damage, and longer
                               durations add whole ticks. Leave it out for no burn.
    impact: 46.0               optional, GroundArea spells only: every enemy inside
                               the area as it lands takes this much at once, before its
                               ticks. It grows with the spell's damage. Leave it out for
                               no impact.


3. HOW IT LOOKS, AND HOW FAST IT FLIES
-------------------------------------

  look_spell is the number of a real WoW spell. The game shows that spell's casting, missile,
  impact and sound. Look the number up on any vanilla spell database, for example:

      Fireball 133      Frostbolt 116      Shadow Bolt 686      Frost Nova 122

  If that WoW spell is a missile, your spell's missile flies at the same speed as in WoW
  (Fireball 24 yards per second, Frostbolt 28, Shadow Bolt 20, Arcane Shot 40). The damage
  lands exactly when the missile arrives on screen. If the WoW spell is not a missile, the hit
  is instant.


4. THE 11 WAYS A SPELL CAN WORK (delivery)
-----------------------------------------

    Projectile          shoots at bosses and elites first, then the nearest enemies (a
                        wounded enemy in a tight bunch around the nearest one goes first)
    Chain               hits random nearby enemies one after another
    Nova                hits everything around you
    GroundArea          a lasting area that hits again and again
    Bounce              jumps from enemy to enemy (like Chain Lightning)
    Melee               hits bosses and elites first, then the closest enemies (picked the
                        same way as projectiles)
    DamageOverTime      a poison, curse or bleed that keeps hurting. It goes to bosses
                        first, then elites, then the healthiest enemies. One cast puts at
                        most one on each enemy, so cast after cast the first in line fills
                        up before the next one gets any. A spell that hits several enemies
                        puts one on each of the first ones in line. An enemy that is already
                        full only gets it again when it would wear off before your next
                        cast; otherwise the next enemy in line does.
    Finisher            a melee hit that does double damage to badly hurt enemies
    Cone                a cone in front of you
    SelfEffect          a heal, shield, buff or immunity on yourself
    Totem               puts down a totem near enemies, in front of you (to your left
                        when a milestone casts it). It strikes half a second after
                        landing, then every 2 seconds for as long as it lasts (a longer
                        duration means more strikes).

  Which numbers matter:
      projectile, chain, bouncing chain, melee, finisher: damage, cooldown, range, targets
      nova, cone: damage, cooldown, radius
      ground area: damage, cooldown, range, radius, duration, ticks
      over time: damage, cooldown, range, duration, ticks
      self: cooldown, duration, and its effect
      totem: pulse_spell, cooldown, radius, duration (and damage if it attacks);
             ticks does nothing for totems


5. EXTRA EFFECTS (effect)
-------------------------

      None                                  nothing extra
      Slow(amount: 0.5, seconds: 4.0)       slows enemies by 50% for 4 seconds
      Root(seconds: 3.0)                    enemies cannot move
      Stun(seconds: 2.0)                    enemies cannot move or attack
      Fear(seconds: 3.0)                    enemies run away from you
      Heal(share: 0.15)                     heals you for 15% of your health
      Shield(share: 0.3, seconds: 10.0)     a shield worth 30% of your health
      DamageBuff(amount: 0.2, seconds: 8.0) +20% damage for 8 seconds
      HasteBuff(amount: 0.2, seconds: 8.0)  faster cooldowns for 8 seconds
      Immunity(seconds: 3.0)                you take no damage
      (and a few more, listed in spells.md)


6. WHAT LEVEL-UP CARDS CAN IMPROVE (upgrades)
---------------------------------------------

      Power      more damage or healing
      Cooldown   casts more often
      Targets    hits more enemies
      Area       bigger
      Duration   lasts longer

  List the ones that fit your spell. The game also skips any that make no sense for its
  delivery (a nova cannot get more targets, for example).


7. WHAT KIND OF SPELL IT IS (categories) - CHANGING A WHOLE KIND AT ONCE
-----------------------------------------------------------------------

  Every spell says what kind of spell it is. A spell can be several kinds at once: Frost Nova
  is DirectDamage, AreaOfEffect and CrowdControl.

      DirectDamage     hits for damage straight away
      DamageOverTime   hurts again and again over time (poisons, curses, bleeds, lasting
                       areas, attacking totems)
      AreaOfEffect     hits everything in an area (novas, lasting areas, cones)
      Melee            hits up close
      Ranged           hits from a distance (missiles, chains)
      CrowdControl     slows, roots, stuns or scares enemies
      Sustain          heals you
      Shield           puts a shield on you
      DamageReduction  makes you take less damage (shields, immunity, weakening enemies)
      Reflect          hurts enemies that hit you (like Thorns)
      Buff             more damage or speed for a while
      Summon           puts down a totem

  WHY: to change a whole kind of spell at once. Open content\spell_categories.ron. It has
  one line per kind, with numbers that are all 1.0:

      (category: DamageOverTime, damage: 1.0, cooldown: 1.0, range: 1.0, radius: 1.0,
       duration: 1.0, effect: 1.0, effect_seconds: 1.0),

  Every number multiplies ALL spells of that kind: 1.2 = 20% more, 0.8 = 20% less.
  All 1.0 leaves every spell at its own numbers.

      damage          how much it hurts (for effects over time: in total)
      cooldown        time between casts (smaller = more often)
      range           how far it reaches
      radius          how big its area is
      duration        how long the spell lasts (areas, poisons, totems, and buffs or
                      shields on you). Ticks stay the same size, so a poison that lasts
                      longer also hurts more in total.
      effect          how strong its extra effect is (slow, heal, shield, buff ...)
      effect_seconds  how long its extra effect holds an enemy (stun, root, fear, slow)

  Example - all poisons, curses and bleeds 10% stronger and 20% longer:

      (category: DamageOverTime, damage: 1.1, duration: 1.2),

  (numbers you leave out count as 1.0). A spell that is several kinds gets every line that
  fits it, multiplied together.

  For a NEW spell, list the kinds that fit it; every spell needs at least one. Talents can
  also be made to react to a kind of spell (see classes.md).


7a. WHAT THE SPELL IS ABOUT (tags)
----------------------------------

  Every spell also has tags, for example:

      tags: [Nuke, Sustain]

  A spell that hurts enemies gets exactly ONE of these, by its shape:

      Level1     the class's starting spell
      Nuke       hits 1 enemy
      Cleave     hits 2 to 5 enemies
      Dot        hurts over time (poisons, curses, bleeds)
      Nova       hits everything around you, or in a cone in front of you
      Area       a lasting area on the ground
      Pet        a creature fights for you (totems)

  On top of that (or alone, for spells that do no damage):

      Sustain    it heals you
      Buffs      it gives you more damage or speed for a while
      Defensive  shields, immunity, hurting enemies that hit you, weakening enemies

  WHY: each tag has a number in content\balance.ron, under "spell_tags", all 1.0:

      spell_tags: (
          level_1: 1.0, nuke: 1.0, cleave: 1.0, dot: 1.0, nova: 1.0, area: 1.0, pet: 1.0,
          sustain: 1.0, buffs: 1.0, defensive: 1.0,
      ),

  Each number changes only what its tag is about: a damage tag the damage, sustain how much
  it heals, buffs how big the buff is, defensive how big the shield, the weakening or the
  hurt-back damage is. Cooldowns and how long things last never change. The pet number also
  changes the damage of every pet a talent gives you. Example - every nuke 10% stronger:
  nuke: 1.1. Restart the game after a change.

  The full list of which spell has which tags is in spells.md, section 8a. For a NEW spell,
  pick its tags the same way; the tests check every spell. Race spells get no tags: tune
  their numbers one by one.


8. WHAT YOU GET FOR FREE
------------------------

  - A legendary amulet can remake your new spell, like any other.
  - Talents can boost it by its id, by its school, or by its kind.
  - The lines in spell_categories.ron apply to it as soon as it lists that kind.
  - The tag numbers in balance.ron apply to it as soon as it lists that tag.
  - Coloured spell cards make it start stronger.
